# Cadence design brief
Audience: Arabic-speaking professional English learners. Calm, articulate, quietly premium.
Concept: An English atelier, with a speaking desk rather than an academic test.
Animation mode: non-animated — user prioritised clear, fast, responsive interactive practice.
Palette: cool paper #f5f8f6, evergreen #214f41, mint grey #e8f0eb.
Typography: Manrope display / DM Sans body / Georgia English reading excerpts.
Sections: workspace header, compact introductory hero, disclosure, mode selector, two-panel training desk, libraries, progress, journal.
Interaction: user-operated voice recording, British speech synthesis, transcripts, word similarity indicator, persisted edits and local notes.
Accessibility: RTL/LTR, high contrast, labels, responsive panels, reduced motion.
Data: clearly fictional lesson fixtures. Local-only data with export/import and no accuracy claims.
