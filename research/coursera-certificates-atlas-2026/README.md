## Logo coverage: 39/39 without blank images
All 185 logo-image occurrences in the generated HTML are embedded as data resources. The PDF is rendered directly from the same self-contained HTML. All 39 provider profiles have a publicly published graphic and the audit reports **zero unresolved provider artworks**.

By source category, there are:
- 18 published full-colour brand SVGs replacing the earlier monochrome icons.
- 7 other published brand/logo images.
- 5 logos located on the organizations' own sites.
- 1 official site icon.
- 8 official-site or published favicon graphics used where a full wordmark was not located or verified.

**Important:** A favicon is an organization's genuine public brand icon, **not** a substitute for a full corporate wordmark. See `logo-audit.json` for the exact logo/image, source URL, type, and provenance for each issuer. No unverified illustration has been passed off as an official logo.

The original 18 monochrome marks were replaced with brand-colour vectors sourced from the theSVG public brand repository (https://github.com/glincker/thesvg). The SkillUp Online logo is a published image displayed by an IBM SkillsBuild community. Other brands were retrieved from public SVG repositories, sites operated by the named organizations, or their public site icons.
